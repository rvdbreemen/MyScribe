### Open me first.txt
MyScribe is not signed with an Apple certificate yet.

Drag MyScribe to Applications, then start it once with a right-click
> Open, or open System Settings > Privacy & Security and press
"Open Anyway" after the first attempt.

The first start downloads the speech engine (about 1 GB on a Mac) and
shows its progress. Later starts skip that.
