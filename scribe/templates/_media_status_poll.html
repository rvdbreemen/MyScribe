{# What the status cell's own poll answers with (TASK-050). The cell it replaces, and beside it the folder and the labels as out-of-band swaps. This rides the poll that already exists rather than adding a timer: the status cell asks for itself every two seconds for exactly as long as the job is queued or running, which is exactly as long as there is something new to show. When the job reaches a terminal status the fragment comes back without polling attributes and everything stops on its own, the final labels having arrived with that last answer. Two cells travel instead of the table for the reason _media_status.html gives: swapping #media-table would close an open row menu and throw away a half-typed rename, and a run is exactly when someone is tidying the rest of the library.
