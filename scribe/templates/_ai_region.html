{# The AI region: a menu of what this recording can be asked, a tab per answer, and one panel showing the tab you chose (TASK-053.04). Robert, after the first three steps landed: "Het moet wel heel eenvoudig benaderbaar zijn, de kern is en blijft het transcript. Wellicht is het handiger om een menu bovenaan het scherm te hebben, met de verschillende opties. En een soort van tabbladen of iets dergelijks om snel te schakelen tussen de verschillende kaarten." Three things carry the weight, and each answers a way this could go wrong. **It sits ABOVE the transcript and OUTSIDE #transcript-panel.** Above, because an answer thirty thousand pixels below the button that asked for it is the fault this rebuild exists to remove. Outside, because #transcript-panel swaps its own outerHTML after a rename, a move or a word correction - and an answer being written must survive that. Being a sibling before it is what keeps both true at once. **Every panel is rendered; all but one are hidden.** `hx-trigger="every 2s"` only fires for an element that is in the document, so a card left out to save markup would stop polling and its answer would never arrive. Hidden is not absent. This is also why the tab strip shows which kinds are working: the poll is running whether or not you are looking at it. **A button activates its own tab before it posts.** `scrollIntoView` and htmx's `show:` are no-ops inside a hidden container, so asking a question while another tab is open would put the answer somewhere nobody can see - the same bug as the thirty thousand pixels, one layer up. app.js switches the tab on click, before the request leaves. Context is `ai` from scribe.web.ai_ui.panel_context; `ai.selected` is the kind to open on, which the browser then overrides from what was open last time for this recording. #}
