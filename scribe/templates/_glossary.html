Glossary

Names this machine should get right. Each term is used twice: it biases the decoder while it transcribes, and afterwards a correction pass fixes what came out close but not right. Corrections are stored separately from the words - nothing is ever overwritten, and removing a term takes its corrections back out on the next re-run. The heaviest terms go first; the decoder only takes about {{ hotword_limit }} tokens' worth.
