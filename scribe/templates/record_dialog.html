{# The record dialog: a microphone, the transcription options, a folder. Its own dialog rather than a tab in Transcribe, because recording is a different act from handing over a file, and the three file doors beside it were three ways of saying "not this". The recorder starts as the dialog opens (data-record-autostart, honoured by recorder.js): a person who pressed Record has said what they want, and the browser's own permission prompt is still the consent gate. There is no submit button in the footer on purpose. The recording is finished by the recorder's own "Stop and transcribe", which posts this form's fields to /record/{session}/finish - so language, tier, speakers, folder and the name travel with the audio. The form has no action and no submit button, and data-enter-scope keeps Enter from the browser: without recorder.js there is no session and nothing to post, and the hint says so. The options are the same partial the transcribe dialog uses; see _transcribe_options.html for why. #} {% import "_macros.html" as m %}
